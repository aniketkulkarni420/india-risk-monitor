## Sign convention fix found in v2

`inr_usd` was incorrectly in `INVERSE_FEEDERS` in the v1 conception (high INR/USD = weak rupee = high risk, NOT low risk). Removed. `brent_crude` deliberately kept NON-inverse (framework calibrated for India-as-oil-importer; low oil reads as low risk).

## Framework limitation now documented

The score treats oil-price level as the proxy for oil risk. During global demand-shock events (e.g. 2020 COVID) where oil COLLAPSED, the oil driver reads LOW even though the demand-shock signaled broader economic stress. This is a known calibration trade-off — the framework is built for India-as-importer, not for global recession signaling. COVID scenario in backtest reads 60 (not 95) for this reason.
