Why this works

One focus
Risk Score (50) is the only thing at hero size. Everything else is supporting. Eye lands in <50ms.
One accent color
Burnt orange used for: the status word, the elevated driver bar, the chart line, the "elevated" driver number. Used nowhere else. Color carries meaning.
Typography spans 14× ratio
144px score · 56px driver number · 32px section · 17px context · 13px status · 11px labels · 10px eyebrow. The ratio itself is the hierarchy.
Whitespace IS the design
80px between hero and drivers. 96px between drivers and chart. 64px before peek. Premium = breathing room, not density.
Numbers, not narration
One sentence of context. No journalism. No "Strait still closed; tanker rates retreating..." — that's a news article.
One chart, max
The 90-day pulse. Faint gridlines, no axes, one dot, one number. Apple Stocks aesthetic.
Sidebar as spine
Edge nav, 88px wide, persistent. Active state is one 2px line, not a colored block. Out of the way until needed.
Progressive disclosure
All v2 telemetry (triple-jeopardy, regime/shock, correlation adj, reference events) still exists — accessed by clicking the score. Home doesn't shove it in your face.
