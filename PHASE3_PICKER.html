My recommended build order · ship over 2 sessions

  1. Session A (~10h · one focused day): Real Econ chips → Market overlay → Sectors grid. Ships the 3 P1s. Highest user-visible impact for least scaffold work.
  2. Session B (~7-8h · split over 2 days): Inline event markers (1h) → Macro V60 toggle (7h). Or skip Macro and do Mobile pass (6h) — depends on whether you want polish or reach.
  3. Defer: Saved tabs (3h), 24h Feed (8h), Peer Compare (12h). Revisit after retail/HNI feedback on what's already live.
